Recorded sounds in this folder
==============================

Many of Orbit's sounds (footsteps, doors, cloth, coins, mining, the shuttle's
engines, the interface cues and the whole casino) are made from recordings by
Kenney (www.kenney.nl), from these five packs:

  - Casino Audio
  - Impact Sounds
  - RPG Audio
  - Sci-fi Sounds
  - Interface Sounds

Kenney released them under Creative Commons Zero, CC0 1.0 Universal:
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 puts them in the public domain, so they may be used, changed and shared
for any purpose without asking or giving credit. We credit Kenney anyway,
with thanks.

For Orbit the recordings were trimmed, level-matched, mixed down to mono,
resampled to 22.05 kHz and, for some cues, layered (a pair of footsteps, a
latch followed by a door). Which cue uses which recording is listed in
RECORDED in extensions/orbit/orbit_sounds.py.

The other sounds in this folder are synthesized by orbit_sounds.py and are
part of Hariku, under its licence (GPL-3.0-or-later with the Hariku
Extension Exception).
