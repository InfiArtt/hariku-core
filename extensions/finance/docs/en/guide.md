# Finance

Finance keeps track of the money you earn and spend. It shows your balance, lists your
transactions by month and category, warns you when a monthly budget runs low, and records
regular payments such as rent or salary by itself. It needs Hariku 2.4 or newer.

## Getting started

In Hariku's main window, press K to open Finance, or Shift+K to note down an expense
quickly. You can also open Aruna (Ctrl+Alt+Backspace) and type "finance" or "add an
expense".

Everything stays on your computer. Finance works offline and sends nothing over the
internet.

## Noting an expense quickly

1. Press Shift+K. The "Add expense" window opens on "Amount".
2. Type the amount, for example 25000, 25.000 or 25rb.
3. In "Category", type a category or pick one with the arrow keys, such as Food or
  Transport. If you leave it empty, the expense goes under Other.
4. Add a description if you like, then press Enter.

Hariku says what it saved, for example "Expense Rp 25.000, Food, saved". The expense is
dated today. To record income, or an expense on another day, use the Finance window.

## Writing amounts

Amounts are whole numbers, written the way you would on paper:

- 20000, 20.000 or 20,000
- Rp 20.000, or Rp 20.000,-
- 20rb, 20 ribu or 20k for thousands
- 1,5jt or 1.5 juta for millions

## The Finance window

Press K. The window's title tells you your balance, and the focus is on the list of
transactions.

- "Summary", at the top, lists your balance, today's income and expenses, this month's,
  and all time's, and the totals of what the list shows now.
- "Month" and "Category" narrow the list, for example to September's food expenses.
  "All months" and "All categories" show everything again.
- "Transactions" lists the newest first, one full sentence per row: "23 Sep 2026,
  Expense, Food, Rp 20.000, coffee". A transaction recorded from a recurring one ends
  with "recurring".

Escape closes the window.

## Adding, changing and deleting transactions

- To add one, press "Add". Fill in "Type" (Expense or Income), "Amount", "Category",
  "Description" (optional) and "Date", then press Enter. The date starts as today; type
  another as YYYY-MM-DD, such as 2026-09-03, or as 03/09/2026.
- To change one, select it in the list and press Enter, or "Edit".
- To delete one, select it and press Delete, or the "Delete" button, then confirm.

"Category" offers, in alphabetical order, the categories you have used and ready-made
ones: Food, Transport, Bills, Rent, Shopping, Health, Education, Entertainment, Salary,
Gift, Investment and Other. You can also type a new one.

## Budgets

A budget is a monthly limit on what you spend in one category, such as Rp 1.500.000 for
Food.

1. In the Finance window, press "Budgets".
2. Press "Set budget", choose the "Category", type the "Monthly limit", and press Enter.

The list shows this month: for each budget, how much you have spent, in money and in
percent, and what is left or how much you went over. Categories you spent on without a
budget are listed too; select one and press Enter to give it a budget. Enter on a budget
changes it, and Delete (or "Remove") removes it.

When an expense you record for this month takes a category past 80% of its budget, Hariku
warns you right after saving it: "Warning: Food budget 85% used, Rp 1.275.000 of
Rp 1.500.000." It tells you again when you reach 100%.

## Recurring transactions

Use these for money that comes or goes on a schedule, such as rent, a subscription or
your salary.

1. In the Finance window, press "Recurring", then "Add".
2. Fill in "Type", "Amount", "Category" and "Description" as for any transaction.
3. In "Repeats", choose Daily, Weekly, Monthly or Yearly. In the next field, say every
  how many days, weeks, months or years; 1 means every one.
4. In "Starts on", type the first date as YYYY-MM-DD. Keep "Active" ticked and press
  Enter.

Hariku records each transaction on its day: when Hariku starts, when the date changes,
and when you open Finance, and says "Recurring transactions recorded: 1". If Hariku was
closed that day, it catches up the next time it runs. A start date in the past records
every date from then until today straight away. A monthly transaction keeps its day: one
that starts on the 31st comes on the last day of shorter months.

Each row of the "Recurring transactions" list gives the schedule, the amount and the next
date. "Pause or resume" stops one for a while; when you resume it, the dates you missed
are skipped. Enter changes the selected one and Delete deletes it. The transactions it
has already recorded stay in your history.

## Settings

Open Preferences (Ctrl+P) and go to the Finance page.

- "Currency symbol": what comes before every amount. It is Rp at first; leave it empty
  for none.
- "Remind me once a day to record expenses if I haven't yet": off at first. When it is
  on and you haven't recorded anything yourself that day by the reminder time, Hariku
  says "Reminder: you haven't recorded any expenses today." and shows a notification.
  Recurring transactions don't count.
- "Reminder time (HH:MM, 24-hour)": 20:00 at first.

Press OK to save.

## Keys and commands

- K: open Finance.
- Shift+K: quickly add an expense.
- In the Finance lists: Enter changes the selected item, Delete deletes or removes it,
  and Escape closes the window.

K and Shift+K work in Hariku's main window. You can change them in Preferences, Input
Gestures, under Finance, and make them global there, so they work outside Hariku too.

In Aruna (Ctrl+Alt+Backspace), "finance", "open finance", "balance", "keuangan" or
"saldo" opens Finance, and "add an expense", "new expense", "catat pengeluaran" or "tambah
pengeluaran" opens the quick expense.
