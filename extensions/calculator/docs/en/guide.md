# Calculator & Converter

Calculate, convert units and currencies, flip a coin, roll dice, split a bill and work out
a discount, by typing or saying it to Aruna in English or Indonesian. It needs Hariku 2.9
or newer. Everything is worked out on your computer; only currency rates are fetched
online.

## Getting started

Install Calculator & Converter from the Extension Store. Open Aruna, Hariku's command bar,
with Ctrl+Alt+Backspace, type a sum such as "25 x 4" or "what's 15% of 80" and press
Enter. With the Voice Control extension you can say it instead.

The answer is spoken and shows in Aruna's "Last result". Numbers are written the way
Hariku's language writes them: "33.33" and "1,250,000" in English, "33,33" and "1.250.000"
in Indonesian. The exact wording can change a little with Hariku's speaking style.

From Hariku 2.11 you can type the sum or conversion on its own, as in the examples below.
On Hariku 2.9 and 2.10, start with a word such as "what's", "calculate" or "convert" (see
On Hariku 2.9 and 2.10).

## Sums

Some examples:

- "25 x 4", "(3 + 4) * 2", "100 divided by 3", "2 to the power of 10"
- "square root of 2", "cube root of 27", "7 factorial", "remainder of 17 divided by 5"
- "what's 15% of 80", "350 + 12%"
- "berapa 25 kali 4", "akar 144", "2 pangkat 10", "sisa bagi 17 dan 5"

Aruna says the sum back in words, so you hear it if a number was misheard: "25 times 4 =
100." When the result had to be rounded, it says so: "100 divided by 3 = about 33.33."
Very large results are said in powers of ten.

### Numbers in words

You can type or say numbers in digits or in words, the way Voice Control writes them:
"twenty-five", "one and a half", "a hundred", "3.5 million", "dua puluh lima", "seratus
dua belas", "setengah", "satu koma lima", "2 juta", "1,5 miliar", "350 ribu". Digits are
read the way Hariku's language writes them: in English "2.5" is two and a half and "1,250"
is one thousand two hundred and fifty.

### Percent

Percent works as on a pocket calculator:

- "12% of 350" is 42.
- "350 + 12%" is 392, and "350 - 12%" takes 12% off.
- "20 is what percent of 80" or "20 itu berapa persen dari 80" says 25%.

## Going on from the last result

For five minutes after an answer you can go on from it:

- "plus 5", "times two", "the result divided by 3", "tambah 5", "kali dua", "hasilnya
  dibagi 3". A result with a unit keeps it: after "2 feet in inches", "times 2" gives 48
  inches.
- "what was the result" or "berapa hasilnya" says it again.
- "copy the result" or "salin hasilnya" copies it to the clipboard, just the number.

A sentence that starts with a word like "plus", "minus" or "times" in those five minutes
goes on from the last result. To start a new sum with a negative number, type it with the
sign, as in "-3 x 2".

## Units

Put the amount, its unit and the unit you want: "2 feet in inches", "5 km to miles", "100
fahrenheit to celsius", "70 kg in pounds", "60 mph to km per hour", "how many ml in a
cup", "1 hectare in square meters", "1 GB in MB", "2 kaki berapa inci", "3 jam berapa
menit". Two units together work both ways: "5 feet 11 inches to cm", "170 cm to feet and
inches".

You can convert length, weight, volume, cooking measures, temperature (Celsius,
Fahrenheit, Kelvin, Réaumur), speed, area, time, data sizes, pressure, energy, fuel
economy (km/l, mpg, l/100 km) and angles. Some measures to know:

- A cup is 240 ml, a tablespoon 15 ml and a teaspoon 5 ml.
- Indonesian units: an ons is 100 grams, a kuintal 100 kilograms, an are 100 square metres
  and a tumbak 14 square metres.
- A year counts as 365 days, and a month as a twelfth of a year.
- Calories are food calories, that is kilocalories.
- KB, MB and GB follow your setting (1024 or 1000 per step); KiB, MiB and GiB are always
  1024, and bits always 1000.

Aruna adds a short note where an answer could be misread, for example which KB, MB and GB
it used or how big an ons is.

### Words with two meanings

The other unit in the sentence settles it:

- "kilo" is kilograms, or kilometres next to a length such as miles.
- "ounce" is weight, or fluid ounces next to a volume.
- "degrees" and "derajat" are Celsius, or Fahrenheit when you convert to Celsius, or an
  angle next to radians.

If you give an amount without a unit to convert to ("what's 5 km"), Aruna asks which unit
you want.

## Money

- "100 dollars to rupiah", "50 euro to yen", "1 juta rupiah dalam dolar", "Rp 1,5 juta ke
  dolar singapura".
- "how much is 100 dollars" or "100 dolar berapa": with no second currency, it goes to
  your home currency. If the amount is already in your home currency, it goes to US
  dollars, or to euros when your home currency is the dollar.
- "exchange rate of the euro" or "kurs dolar": one of that currency in your home currency.

The answer gives the date of the rates: "100 US dollars = 1,790,855 rupiah, at the rates
of 25 September." About 35 currencies are known, among them the rupiah, US dollar, euro,
yen, Singapore dollar, ringgit, riyal, pound, yuan and won.

The rates are the central banks' daily reference rates. They are fetched the first time
you convert money and kept for a few hours. When you are offline, rates up to a week old
are used; without any, Aruna says it can't get them.

## Coins, dice, random numbers, bills and discounts

- "flip a coin" or "lempar koin".
- "roll a die", "roll 2 dice", "roll a d20", "lempar dadu", "lempar 2 dadu": 1 to 10 dice,
  with 2 to 1000 sides; several dice also give the total.
- "random number between 1 and 6", "angka acak 1 sampai 100".
- "split 120 dollars 3 ways", "bagi tagihan 350 ribu untuk 4 orang": each person's share,
  for 1 to 1000 people. Add a tip: "... tip 10%".
- "30% off 250", "diskon 30% dari 250 ribu": the price after the discount and how much you
  save.

## On Hariku 2.9 and 2.10

Before Hariku 2.11, Aruna only passes a sentence to the calculator when it starts with a
word such as "what is", "what's", "calculate", "convert", "how much", "how many",
"berapa", "hitung" or "konversi", or ends with "berapa":

- "what's 25 x 4", "convert 2 feet to inches", "berapa inci 2 kaki", "100 dolar berapa".
- To go on from the last result, start with "the result" or "hasilnya": "the result times
  two", "hasilnya tambah 5".
- "square root of ...", "... factorial", "sisa bagi ...", "lempar ...", "angka acak ...",
  "random number ..." and "bagi tagihan ..." work as they are.

From Hariku 2.11 these words are no longer needed, but they still work. A bare sum that
looks like a date, a time or a phone number, such as "25/12" or "10:30", isn't taken as a
sum; start it with "what's" or "berapa".

## Settings

Open Preferences (Ctrl+P in Hariku's window) and choose Calculator & Converter.

- Home currency: Automatic (rupiah when Hariku speaks Indonesian, else US dollar), or one
  of 21 currencies.
- Decimal places: Automatic (2 when a result is rounded; exact results are said as they
  are), or 0 to 6.
- Data sizes (KB, MB, GB): 1024 per step, as Windows counts (the default), or 1000 per
  step, as drive makers count.
- Copy each result to the clipboard: off at first.

The settings are saved when you press OK or Apply.

## Keys and commands

These have no key by default. You can give them one in Preferences, Input Gestures, where
they are under "Calculator"; tick "Make this shortcut Global" so the key works in every
window.

- Copy the last calculator result: "copy the result", "copy the answer", "salin hasilnya".
- Say the last calculator result: "last result", "what was the result", "hasil terakhir".
- Say a random number from 1 to 100: "random number", "pick a number", "angka acak".
- Flip a coin: "flip a coin", "toss a coin", "heads or tails", "lempar koin".
- Roll a die: "roll a die", "roll the dice", "lempar dadu", "kocok dadu".

## Privacy

Sums, units, coins, dice, random numbers, bills and discounts are worked out on your
computer, with rules built into Hariku, not AI or an online service. What you type or say
isn't stored; only the last result is kept in memory for five minutes. It goes to the
clipboard only when you ask, or after every answer if you turn that on.

Only currency conversions go online. The first time you convert money in a while, Hariku
downloads the day's reference rates of all currencies at once from Frankfurter
(frankfurter.dev), a free service with no account. The request is the same for everybody:
it carries no currency, no amount and nothing about you. The rates are saved on your
computer with the time they were fetched.
