# Lumina

Birthday reminders for friends, family and yourself. When Hariku starts, Lumina tells you
about birthdays a week, three days and a day ahead and on the day itself, and it can tell
you about birthdays as you move through the calendar.

## Getting started

Press Ctrl+Shift+B in Hariku's main window to open "Lumina — Birthday List". Lumina's
settings are on the Lumina page of Preferences.

## Adding a birthday

1. In the list, press "Add Birthday".
2. Type the person's name at "Name:".
3. Type the day and month at "Birth Date (DD-MM):", such as 25-06 for 25 June.
4. If you know it, type the year at "Birth Year (optional):". Then Lumina says on the day
   how old they turn.
5. If you like, type your own words at "Custom greeting (optional):". Lumina says them on
   the day instead of its usual happy birthday.
6. Press "Save".

Without a name or a valid date, Lumina says "Invalid date format. Use DD-MM (e.g. 25-06)."
and keeps the window open.

## The birthday list

The list is sorted by name. Each line has the name, the date, the year if there is one,
and how many days are left, such as "| in 12 days", or "TODAY!" on the day. Your own
birthday comes first, with a star and "(Me)".

- "Edit Birthday" opens the selected birthday to change it.
- "Delete Birthday" asks, for example, "Delete Rina's birthday?" and deletes it if you say
  yes.
- "Cancel" closes the list.

Your own birthday can't be edited here; it comes from your profile.

## Your own birthday

Lumina takes your birthday, and the year if you gave one, from Preferences, Profile, and
calls you by the name Hariku uses for you. It has its own words for you, such as "Tomorrow
is your birthday, Rafli! Are you ready to celebrate?". On the day itself, when Hariku
greets you at startup, Hariku already wishes you a happy birthday, so Lumina doesn't say
it again.

## When the reminders come

A few seconds after Hariku starts, Lumina says each birthday that is exactly 7 days, 3
days or 1 day away, or today, such as "Rina's birthday is in 3 days." or "Tomorrow is
Rina's birthday!". On the day, it adds the age when it knows the year: "Rina turns 30
today."

Lumina checks only when Hariku starts. If Hariku doesn't start on one of those days, that
reminder is skipped.

## Upcoming birthdays

The action "Check Upcoming Birthdays" says the birthdays of the next 30 days, soonest
first, yours included. When there are none, it says "No birthdays in the next 30 days." It
has no key: ask Aruna, or give it one in Input Gestures.

## Birthdays in the calendar

The setting "When selecting a birthday on the calendar:" decides what happens when you
move to a date that is someone's birthday:

- "Announce it out loud" (the default): Hariku says "Birthday:" and the names.
- "Show it on the agenda list": the birthday is the first item of that date's agenda, the
  list you open with Space.
- "Do nothing".

## Settings

In Preferences, Lumina:

- "My Birthday": a reminder that your own birthday is set in Preferences, Profile.
- "Remind on Hariku startup": on at first. When it's off, Lumina gives no reminders when
  Hariku starts.
- "Remind 7 days before", "Remind 3 days before", "Remind 1 day before" and "Remind on the
  day": which of the startup reminders you hear. All are on at first.
- "When selecting a birthday on the calendar:": see Birthdays in the calendar.

## Keys and commands

- Ctrl+Shift+B, in Hariku's main window: Open Birthday List.
- Check Upcoming Birthdays: no key.

Both are under "Lumina" in Preferences, Input Gestures, where you can change or add keys.
Aruna runs them by their names: "open birthday list" or "check upcoming birthdays".
