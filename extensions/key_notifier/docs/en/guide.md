# Key Notifier

Key Notifier plays a sound when you turn Caps Lock or Num Lock on or off. While the key
stays on, it repeats a short sound, so you notice before you type a whole line in
capitals.

## Getting started

It works as soon as it is installed, for Caps Lock. The sounds come with the extension.
To change what it listens to, or its sounds, open Preferences, Key Notifier.

## Settings

Preferences, Key Notifier:

- "Monitor CapsLock": play the sounds for Caps Lock. On by default.
- "Monitor NumLock": play the same sounds for Num Lock. Off by default.
- "ON Sound" and "OFF Sound": what plays when the key is turned on, and when it is
  turned off. Type the path of a WAV file, or press "Browse for the ON sound..." or
  "Browse for the OFF sound..." and choose one.
- "Enable Looping Sound": while a key you monitor stays on, the "Loop Sound" plays again
  and again. On by default. Choose your own loop sound with "Browse for the loop
  sound...".
- "Loop Interval (seconds)": how often the loop sound plays, from 2 to 60 seconds. The
  default is 2.

Press OK to save. If a sound field is empty, or its file is gone, Windows' standard beep
plays instead.
