# World Clock

World Clock tells you the time in cities around the world. It works offline, from the
time zone information that comes with Hariku. It needs Hariku 2.5 or newer.

## Getting started

In Hariku's main window, press W, and Hariku says the time in each of your cities. At
first they are Jakarta, London, New York and Tokyo; you choose your own in Preferences.

World Clock's windows and messages are in English only.

## Hearing the times

W reads every city in turn, with the day of the week: "World clock. Jakarta: Friday 8:44
PM. Tokyo: Friday 10:44 PM." When a city's date is a day ahead of yours, or a day behind,
"(tomorrow)" or "(yesterday)" follows its time.

## The World Clock window

To go through the times one by one, open the World Clock window. It has no key at first:
give it one in Input Gestures (see below), or type "open the world clock window" in Aruna
(Ctrl+Alt+Backspace).

The "Current times" list has one city per row and updates every second while the window
is open. "Speak All" reads every city, "Refresh" updates the list at once, and "Close" or
Escape closes the window.

## Adding and removing cities

1. Open Preferences (Ctrl+P) and go to the World Clock page.
2. Press "Add City".
3. In "City label (what you'll hear)", type the name you want to hear, such as Mecca or
  Mum's place.
4. In "Time zone (type to search)", type the time zone's full name, or pick it with the
  arrow keys. Names are written region first, then city: Asia/Riyadh, Europe/Amsterdam,
  America/Los_Angeles. They must match a name in the list exactly.
5. Press OK. If you left the label empty, the city part of the time zone is used, such
  as Riyadh.
6. Press OK in Preferences to save.

New cities go to the end of the "Cities" list, and W reads them in that order. To remove
a city, select it in the list and press "Remove Selected"; it doesn't ask first, but
nothing is saved until you press OK, so Cancel brings it back.

## Settings

On the World Clock page in Preferences:

- "Use 24-hour time": times such as 20:44 instead of 8:44 PM. Off at first.
- "Cities": your cities, each with its time zone, such as "Tokyo (Asia/Tokyo)", with the
  "Add City" and "Remove Selected" buttons.

Press OK to save.

## Keys and commands

- W: speak the world clock times.
- Open the world clock window: no key at first.

W works in Hariku's main window. You can change keys in Preferences, Input Gestures,
under World Clock, and make them global there, so they work outside Hariku too.

In Aruna (Ctrl+Alt+Backspace), "world clock", "world time", "jam dunia" or "waktu dunia"
speaks the times, and the answer also shows in Aruna's "Last result". "Open the world
clock window" opens the window.
